Eight theme directions for the whole product

Each direction is clean, simple and premium in its own way, and they share nothing: different color temperature, typography, shape and mood. Every mockup shows the same real screen (the transcript editor with retention and publishing status), so you can compare them fairly.

Brand personality we designed for

  • Calm and in control: one place for the whole creator workflow
  • Honest: shows what each platform really allows
  • Crafted: audio quality is the origin story
  • Smart without being loud: AI that tells you what to change

Where the references came from

Mobbin app screens (editors, analytics, inboxes, calendars) and saaslandingpage.com (840 landing pages, AI and dark tags). saaspo.com blocks automated browsers with a bot check, so nothing from it is used here.

What you have today

DM Sans with Instrument Serif and an indigo accent. Themes 2 and 4 are the shortest step from there; the rest are a clean break.

My recommendation

Paper & Ink, with Studio Graphite as its dark mode

The core of Sonicly is reading and editing words, and Paper & Ink makes the transcript feel like a well-set document. It looks premium through typography rather than effects, so it stays calm across busy screens like the calendar, inbox and analytics. It's also the nearest relative of your current serif-plus-sans setup. For dark mode, use Studio Graphite's warm greys rather than inverting the paper. Runner-up: Pearl Glass, if you want to keep the indigo brand color.

Applies to whichever theme you pick

Rules that keep it clean and premium

  1. One accent, used rarely. It marks the primary action, the playhead and the current selection. Everything else is neutral.
  2. Status colors are separate from the accent. Published, processing, failed and scheduled each get a fixed color and a pill shape, so state reads at a glance.
  3. Platform brand colors only as small dots. YouTube red or TikTok black never fill a card or button, or the product turns into a logo wall.
  4. Two typefaces at most, plus a mono for timecodes if the theme has one. Timecodes and numbers use tabular figures.
  5. Spacing on a 4px grid with three card styles at most. Borders separate; shadows are reserved for things that float (menus, modals).
  6. Motion is short and functional: 150–200ms fades and slides, with the playhead and waveform as the only continuous motion.
